# Lesson author prompt

Create one short Chinese lesson matching lesson.schema.json.

Inputs:

- mission: {{mission}}
- learner_profile: {{learner_profile}}
- objective: {{objective}}
- framework_tags: {{framework_tags}}
- source_facts: {{source_facts}}
- relevant_errors: {{relevant_errors}}

Requirements:

- one observable objective;
- 5–20 minutes;
- only necessary explanation;
- two contrasting examples;
- guided production, unaided retrieval, and transfer;
- explicit answer and feedback rules;
- completion evidence;
- source IDs for factual or exam claims;
- no unlicensed reproduction.

Do not treat viewing as mastery. Do not hide the target distinction behind unrelated vocabulary.
