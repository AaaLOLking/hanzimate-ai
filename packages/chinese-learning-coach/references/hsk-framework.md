# HSK Framework

## Version discipline

Never use HSK 3.0 as an unqualified catch-all. Tag content with one framework:

- GF0025-2021: International Chinese Language Education Chinese Proficiency Grading Standards, three stages and nine levels;
- hsk-3.0: the official new HSK syllabus and current three-stage, nine-level test framework;
- current-hsk-1-6: a legacy tag for previously published level-specific syllabi and task formats; preserve it on old content but do not apply it to a new release without checking the current transition policy;
- hsk-7-9: the advanced test and published sample format.

Also tag:

- level;
- skill: listening, speaking, reading, writing, translation;
- linguistic item: syllable, character, word, grammar;
- task type;
- source ID and page;
- content version and reviewer.

## MVP scope

Target HSK 2–4. Keep explanations and distractors within the learner's demonstrated band unless the objective explicitly introduces a new form.

## Content sources

Prefer:

1. official Ministry or Chinese Test standards and samples;
2. licensed teaching material;
3. teacher-reviewed original examples;
4. clearly marked experimental content.

Do not copy substantial textbook or exam passages without permission. Derive new examples and exercises from the skill specification.

## Alignment check

For every lesson or item, verify:

- the objective is observable;
- prerequisites are identified;
- target vocabulary and grammar have a framework tag;
- the answer is defensible;
- distractors test the intended distinction;
- the source supports exam-format claims;
- daily-life claims sound natural in contemporary Mandarin.
