# Lesson Contract

Every published lesson must contain:

- id, slug, version, title;
- framework and level;
- one observable objective;
- prerequisites;
- estimated minutes between 5 and 20;
- target items;
- source citations;
- a short explanation;
- at least two contrasting examples;
- guided practice;
- unaided retrieval practice;
- one transfer task;
- answer and feedback rules;
- completion evidence;
- next-review recommendation.

## Objective test

Good: “在食堂点餐时正确使用‘一份/一碗/一杯’完成三项点餐。”

Bad: “理解量词。”

## Example rules

- Use natural contemporary Mandarin.
- Avoid unexplained vocabulary above the target level.
- Make contrasts differ mainly in the target feature.
- Include pinyin only when it serves the objective.
- Use names and scenarios that do not stereotype nationalities or cultures.

## Practice rules

Recognition can introduce a distinction but cannot complete a lesson. Completion requires production or retrieval evidence.

Avoid answer leakage through:

- visibly different option length;
- grammar hints in formatting;
- repeating the answer immediately before the question;
- implausible distractors.

## Context pack

Include only the Mission fragment, level, objective, lesson knowledge, confirmed relevant errors, demonstrated prerequisites, citations, and teaching policy. Do not include the full learner history.
