# Lesson Quality Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 3.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Objective | absent | vague | observable | observable and mission-linked |
| Level fit | unusable | frequent overload | mostly suitable | controlled and adaptive |
| Accuracy | wrong | uncertain | correct | correct with traceable sources |
| Naturalness | unnatural | several awkward forms | usable | contemporary and context-fit |
| Practice | passive only | recognition only | production | retrieval plus transfer |
| Feedback | absent | generic | specific | immediate and retry-oriented |
| Evidence | none | activity log | valid attempt | delayed/transfer evidence |
| Safety | unsafe | important gaps | safe | safe with clear boundaries |

Approval requires:

- no zero;
- Objective, Accuracy, Practice, and Safety each at least 2;
- total at least 19/24;
- all required sources present.
